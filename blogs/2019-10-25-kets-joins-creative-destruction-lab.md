We are delighted to announce that we have been selected to join the Creative Destruction Lab’s startup programme in Toronto as part of their Quantum Incubator Stream. The Creative Destruction Lab is a seed-stage program for massively scalable, science and technology-based companies. The program employs an objectives-based mentoring process with the goal of maximizing equity-value creation.
